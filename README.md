# Cayley realizations of directed strongly regular graphs on nonabelian groups

**James Elmore and Eric Swartz** (College of William & Mary)

---

We search for $\(n,k,t,\lambda,\mu )$-directed strongly regular graphs (DSRGs) arising as Cayley graphs $\mathrm{Cay}(G,S)$ of **nonabelian** groups $G$. 
Equivalently $S$ is a directed partial difference set, i.e. $S \subseteq G \setminus \{e \}$ and 
```math
S \cdot S = t \cdot e + \lambda \cdot S + \mu \cdot (G - e - S)
```
in the group ring.

Parameter sets come from [Hobart & Brouwer](https://homepages.cwi.nl/~aeb/math/dsrg/dsrg.html)'s DSRG table up to $n \leq 110$,
where $k \leq n/2$.

Searches are conducted via a greedly local search algorithm with random restarts (random-restart hillclimbing or RRHC) and Integer Linear Programs (ILPs).
Nearly all constructions, including at least one construction for every new parameter set realized, were found with RRHC.
The ILP was primarily useful for proving infeasibility.

Of the 549 open parameter sets we checked, we **construct 59** and prove **86 have no Cayley graph construction**.
Of these, 26 construction results were found in parallel with [Wesley & Chung](https://github.com/dualverse-ai/strong_regular_graph), and two were found in parallel with [Gyürki & Klin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16202).

In the following tables,
- **Groups** is the number of nonabelian groups which have a connection set generating the graph,
- **SmallGroup(n,i)** is one representative group's GAP ID, and
- **Connection Set** is the list of indices of elements in that group forming the connection set.
